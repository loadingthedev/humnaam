humnaam हमनाम ہم نام

Decides whether two names written in Latin letters are the same person's name. Built for South Asian and Arabic names, where Mohammed, Muhammad and Mohd are one name.

GitHub npm

01 Search

What you type is scored against names from a public sanctions list. Scores are not percentages: or more is already a likely match, and 1 means the same words.

      02 Compare

      The names are the full names of individuals on the US Treasury OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list, published , used as public test data. A match here is a similarity between names, not an identification of a person.

      Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
